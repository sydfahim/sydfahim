## 💫 About Me

Honestly? Most of the time, the gap between what a brand says it is and how it actually feels is huge. I'm the person who cares about fixing that.

Whether it's the logo, the website, or the whole vibe — it all gotta make sense together.

I'm a designer who can code, which means I don't just throw designs over the wall to engineers and hope for the best. I actually build stuff. I use Figma, HTML, CSS, React — whatever it takes to make it real.

### What I do:

* 🎨 Brand identity that actually sticks — visual language, positioning, and the whole system
* 🧠 UI/UX design that makes sense
* 💻 Frontend development — turning designs into code that works
* 🔍 Figuring out what people actually need before jumping to solutions
